"To Lay Me Down"

 Lyric written in London, 1970. According to an interview with Hunter in a documentary film by Jeremy Marre, "To Lay Me Down," "Brokedown Palace" and "Ripple" were all composed in one afternoon, over a half-bottle of retsina. (The film aired on VH-1 on April 16, 1997.)

Musical details:

  • Key: G
  • Time signature: 3/4 ("Moderately slow Gospel: 9/8 feeling")
  • Chords used: D, C, G, Am, Em, Bm Bm7, A, D7. E, Gsus4, Dsus
  • Songbook availability:
    • Garcia
    • Anthology, vol. II

Recorded on

  • Garcia
  • Reckoning

Covered on Deadicated by The Cowboy Junkies.

First performance: July 30, 1970, at the Matrix, in San Francisco. "To Lay Me Down" opened the show, which consisted of one acoustic set. It was followed by "Dire Wolf". The song's subsequent performance history is fairly unusual among Dead tunes, dropping from the repertoire for 200-300 shows at a time several times.
