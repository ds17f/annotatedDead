"Tons of Steel"

 Recorded on
  • In The Dark

First performance: December 28, 1984, at the Civic Auditorium in San Francisco, California. "Tons" was soundchecked, then appeared in the first set, following "Dire Wolf" and preceding "I Ain't Superstitious." The other first in the show was "Day Tripper." I was there!

See Ken Rattenne's essay, Railroad as Metaphor", for more on the song.
