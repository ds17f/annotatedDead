"Eternity"

 Recorded on
  • So Many Roads

First performance: Sunday, February 21, 1993, at the Oakland Coliseum Arena. "Eternity" appeared in the first set, following "Lazy River Road" (also appearing for the first time at the show) and preceding "Ramble On Rose." Also debuting at the show was "Liberty." The song remained in steady rotation thereafter.
