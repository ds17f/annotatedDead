with my heart in my shoes,
my head hanging down?
Now my only trouble
the rest I forgot
is to show how I love you

Done time in the lockup

and time in defeat

and I know it's a lot
when I say that I love you

and make my best shot
to show how I love you
believe it or not

with a breeze in the pines
and a grey whipoorwill
making music together
that guitars never caught
[in a world time forgot]--alternate line
let me show how I love you
believe it or not

"Believe It Or Not"

 Musical details:
  • Key: C
  • Time signature: 3/4 (Directions: "In one")
  • Chords used: G, E-flat, B-flat, F, D7, G6, Fmaj7,Am, Em, B, Em7, A7, B7

Recorded for Built to Last, but not included.

Songbook availability:

  • Anthology II

First performance: June 23, 1988, at Alpine Valley Music Theatre in East Troy, Wisconsin. "Believe It Or Not" appeared in the second spot in the second set, following "Hey Pocky Way" and preceding "Man Smart, Woman Smarter." The other first in the show was "Blackbird." Performed seven times between its debut and 1990.

Note: Carl Anderson, of Bellingham, Washington, called to say that the song was actually first played on February 26, 1977, at the Swing Auditorium in San Bernardino, California. Any solid evidence out there?
