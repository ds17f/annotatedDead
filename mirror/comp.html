The Annotated Grateful Dead Discography: Compilation and Soundtrack appearances

An installment in The Annotated Grateful Dead Lyrics.
By David Dodd
Kraemer Family Library
University of Colorado at Colorado Springs
Copyright notice; © 1995, David Dodd
This section of the discography lists albums, including soundtracks, which have included music previously recorded by the band, or its members, and music composed specifically for a given soundtrack.
  • Acoustic edge: great acoustic music of the '90s: 1995
    Rhino Records: R2 71969
    Includes "Grateful Dawg": Garcia and Grisman.
  • Arista's perfect 10 rides again: 1985
    Arista: ARCD 8308
    Includes "Alabama Getaway"
  • Classic rock: 1987
    Warner Special Products: 9-27616-2
    Includes "Sugar Magnolia"
  • Delco Electronics Music System Sampler
    Manufactured and produced by Mobile Fidelity Sound Lab. SPCD-8

    Delco Electronics Music System Part No. 16124630

    Contains the Mars Hotel version of "U.S. Blues."

    Note from Gary Hodges, who submitted this info: "I should add it runs either 1 second slower or faster than the Mars Hotel version. The other 11 songs are mostly mid-eighties AOR - Bryan Adams, Huey Lewis, etc. If you would like the entire track listing, let me know."

  • Fillmore: the last days: 1972
    Fillmore: Z3X 31390 (3 sound discs : 33 p1s/b3s rpm, stereo. ; 12 in.)
    Includes notes on the Fillmore and list of performers, Nov. 6, 1965 to July 4, 1971.
    "Words with Bill Graham" (1 disc: 33 p1s/b3s rpm; 7 in.) laid in container.
    Includes "Casey Jones" and "Johnny B. Goode"
    Recorded during performances at the Fillmore in San Francisco, June and July, 1971.
  • Gold & platinum: 1988
    Realm Records: 1T1 7679
    Includes "Touch of Grey"
  • Goodies volume 1: 1972
    Warner Bros: BS 2575
    Includes "Ripple"
  • Greatest rock hits: portrait of a decade, 1975-1985: 1985
    Arista Records: AL 8-8272
    Cover title: Arista's greatest AOR hits : portrait of a decade.
    Includes "Alabama Getaway"
  • Greenpeace: Rainbow Warriors: 1989
    Geffen Records: GHS 24236
    "An album to benefit Greenpeace International."
    Includes "Throwing Stones"
  • Heavy metal: Superstars of the 70's: 1974
    Warner Special Products: SP-2001 (2 sound discs : analog, 33p1s/b3s rpm, stereo. ; 12 in.)
    Contains excerpts of 24 previously released albums by various musical groups, including "Johnny B. Goode" (Grateful Dead)
  • Here it is, the music: vol. 1: 1988
    Rykodisc: CDK 00099
    Includes "Da Birg Song": Garcia and Wales.
  • Highs of the seventies: 1987
    Warner Special Products: 9-27614-2
    Includes "Truckin'"
  • In dreams: 1992
    Sony Music Special Products: A 22837
    Includes selections from Zabriskie Point: "Dark Star" (excerpt) and "Love Scene" by Garcia.
  • Jam: 1994
    Warriors (Basketball team), KFOG
    Includes an otherwise unreleased version of "Bird Song," with Branford Marsalis on saxophone.
  • Looney tunes and merrie melodies: 1970
    Warner Bros. Records: PRO 423
    Includes "Sugar Magnolia"
  • Mask: music from the motion picture soundtrack: 1985
    MCA: MCAC-6140
    Includes "Ripple"
  • Rock anthems: 1986
    K-Tel: TU 1790
    Includes "Truckin'"
  • Rock goes to the movies 1: 1990
    CBS Special Products: AK 46806
    Zabriskie Point selections, including "Dark Star" (excerpt) and "Love scene" by Garcia.
  • Rockmusik: 1978
    Opus Musicum: OM 125-126
    Program notes with historical-analytical commentary by T. Kneif (14 p., ill.)
    Includes "Golden Road"
  • Rude awakening: original motion picture soundtrack: 1989
    Elektra: 60873-2
    Includes Uncle John's Band"
  • Smoke: Motion picture soundtrack: 1995
    PGD/HOLLYWOOD 2061 62024 4
    The Jerry Garcia Band contributes two songs to the soundtrack: "Smoke Gets In Your Eyes," and "Cigarettes and Coffee."
  • Steal this disc 2: 1988
    Rykodisc: RCD 00076
    Includes "Da Birg Song": Garica and Wales.
  • Zabriskie Point: Music from the Motion Picture Soundtrack: 1970
    MGM Records:
    • MGM Special 2354 040 (LP)
    • MGM Cassette 3140 118 (Cassette)
    Relevant tracks:
    • "Excerpt from DARK STAR"--The Grateful Dead (2:30)
    • "Love Scene"--Jerry Garcia (7:04)
