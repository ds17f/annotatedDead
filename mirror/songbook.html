  • Garcia [Songbook]. Garcia, Jerry. New York: Ice Nine Pub. Co ; Warner Bros. 1971.(Music number:VF0070)

  • The Syntax Collector: Some Antics for Piano, A Sleazy Suite Suitable for Salon or Saloon. Constanten, Tom. New York: G. Schirmer, 1987.(Music number:HL50507860)
    [score].
    Contents: "Praalude: The Haight Street Slither"; "That There Old West is Older Now Than Ever," subtitled "An inherently simple concept ... Johannes Brahms returns to the bordello...would you believe, a prairie schooner barcarolle?"; "Lude: Licentious Bicentennial Rag," subtitled "It's that time of the century again ... a look backward, a look forward, perhaps a furtive blance or two sideward ... if you think you recognize anything in this piece, you're probably right, but if you try to nail me down, I'll deny it"; "The Great Green and Gold Take the Cakewalk," subtitled "... a California oak leaf rag ..."; "positivetlude: the San Andreas Stomp," subtitled "...one aspiring akashik librarian's small step toward apotheotic simplicity..." The pieces are characterized by an affinity with the ragtime tradition, and by whimsical instructions to the performer, such as "Walk, don't run," and "If you're short on fingers, the 'grace' notes may be omitted. But keep practicing..." .

  • Classic Grateful Dead: Selections From "American Beauty". Grateful Dead . Secaucus, NJ: Ice Nine/Warner Bros., 1991.(Music number:GF0456)
    [score].
    "Includes complete solos." Contents: "Ripple"; "Truckin'"; "Friend of the Devil"; "Box of Rain"; "Sugar Magnolia." Good attention to detail in the arrangements, with nice touches like "Mandolin (arranged for Guitar)" in "Ripple", and, similarly, pedal steel parts in "Sugar Magnolia" arranged for guitar. Parts are given in standard notation and tab. Vocal harmonies also nicely notated.

  • Hundred Year Hall. Grateful Dead. Authentic Guitar-Tab Edition Miami, FL: Ice Nine/Warner, 1996.(ISBN:1576234398);
    (Music number:PG 9560)
    Contents: "Bertha"; "Big Railroad Blues"*; "China Cat Sunflower"; "Comes a Time"*; "The Other One (Cryptical Envelopment)"*; "Going Down the Road Feelin' Bad"; "I Know You Rider"; "Jack Straw"*; "Me and My Uncle"; "Next Time You See Me"*; "One More Saturday Night"; "Playing in the Band"; "Sugar Magnolia"; "Truckin'"; "Turn On Your Lovelight."* Cover says "Includes complete solos." Detailed tablature transcriptions, but not of complete solos and jams. This is especially noticeable on the Aoxomoxoa version of "China Cat" and the very brief "The Other One" (which, contrary to the advertised "complete solos" has the instruction to "continue soloing freely [11 minutes] approximately"). Asterixed titles above are transcribed from the Hundred Year Hall recording; others are from previewious album versions. Note that the mistakenly-labeled "Cryptical Envelopment" is again so labeled here.
