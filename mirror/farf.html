First performance: March 30, 1980, at the Capitol Theatre in Passaic, New Jersey. "Far From Me" appeared in the first set, following "El Paso," and preceding "Tennessee Jed". It appeared in a fairly regular rotation in the live repertoire until Mydland's death.
